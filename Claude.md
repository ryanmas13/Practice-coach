# Practice Coach

A simple web app (index.html) that helps my son practice viola.
It plays a metronome with quiet "drop-out" bars, records him through
the microphone, and after he presses Stop gives one spoken sentence
of feedback on timing (and tuning for scales). It runs on his iPad
from GitHub Pages, locked with Guided Access.

## How to work with me
- I'm learning to code. Explain your plan in plain English before editing,
  and say which part of index.html each change touches.
- Keep each change small and focused on one feature.
- After editing, summarize what changed and why, in a few sentences.

## Rules for the app
- Everything stays in one file, index.html, as much as possible. No external libraries without my permission and prior explanation of why its needed.
- Keep the kid's screen minimal: one Start/Stop button, the result,
  and the weekly practice line. Settings stay behind the grown-ups question.
- Audio never leaves the device.
- Bump the version label in the bottom-left corner (e.g. v0.6 -> v0.7)
  with every change.
